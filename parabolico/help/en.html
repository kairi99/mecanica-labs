Help guide: projectile motion

Companion of the virtual lab for Practice 2.

You launch a marble and a ping-pong ball from the edge of a table after rolling them down an inclined rail. You time the flight with your own presses, measure the range with the tape measure from the carbon-paper mark and fill Table 2.1. The full help guide (theory of the horizontal launch, how to use the instruments and uncertainties) will come in a later version.
